The workflow

The whole pipeline, in one place

Textile design normally spans four or five tools and a lot of waiting. RIMI AI covers the path from idea to production file without leaving the browser.

01

Capture

Upload a photo, scan or swatch — or describe the print you want and generate options.

02

Build the repeat

Extract a flat tile, heal the seams, and check the repeat at real fabric scale.

03

Develop

Alternate colourways against Pantone, vectorise for screen print, isolate motifs.

04

Present & hand off

Product mockups, 3D garment preview, and a tech pack with the print specification.

Capabilities

What is in the studio

Create

Inspirations

Generate print directions from a brief or a reference, across several models at once, and compare them side by side.

Create

Extract Design

Pull a clean, flat repeat tile out of a photograph — no perspective, no shadows, no background.

Production

Make Seamless

Heal the seams on an existing tile, or generate one that tiles natively. Every result is measured on how closely its edges actually meet.

Production

Repeat & Measurement

Preview the repeat and check the design at true physical scale, with yardage estimates for a given fabric width.

Colour

Colourways

Recolour a design into a full colour story, matched against Pantone TCX rather than eyeballed.

Output

Vectorize

Convert raster artwork to clean vectors for screen printing and engraving, with control over colour count and detail.

Output

Product Mockups

Apply a print to 31 real products across bedding, apparel and accessories — or paint your own mask on any product photo.

Output

3D Garment Preview

See the repeat on a garment in three dimensions before anything is sampled.

Advanced

Layer Studio

Decompose artwork into editable layers, then move, recolour or replace individual motifs and flatten back at full source resolution.

Advanced

Print Advisor

Guidance on print method and cost for a given fabric and production volume, based on the design's actual colour palette.

    The workflow

    Four steps, one browser tab

    Textile design normally spans four or five applications and a great deal of waiting. RIMI AI covers the whole path, from first idea to the file a mill receives.

    01

    Capture

    Upload a photo, scan or swatch — or describe the print and generate directions.

    02

    Build the repeat

    Extract a flat tile, heal the seams, and check the repeat at true fabric scale.

    03

    Develop

    Alternate colourways against Pantone, vectorise for screen print, isolate motifs.

    04

    Hand off

    Product mockups, 3D garment preview, and a tech pack carrying the specification.

    Everything a print needs
    before it reaches the mill

    Create

    Inspirations & Extract

    Generate print directions across several models at once, or pull a clean flat repeat straight out of a photograph — no perspective, no shadows, no background.

    Production

    Make Seamless

    Heal the seams on an existing tile, or generate one that tiles natively. Every result is measured on how closely its edges actually meet — not assumed.

    Colour

    Colourways

    Recolour into a full colour story, matched against Pantone TCX rather than eyeballed.

    Output

    Product Mockups

    Apply a print to 31 real products across bedding, apparel and accessories — or paint your own mask on any product photo.

    Production

    Repeat & Measurement

    Output

    Clean vectors for screen printing and engraving, with control over colour count and detail.

    Decompose artwork into editable layers, move or recolour individual motifs, and flatten back at full source resolution.
